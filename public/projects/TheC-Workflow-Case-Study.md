Portfolio Case Study #2
Thec Workflow

Role: Full-Stack Developer
Status: Live

Overview

TheC Workflow is an internal business operations and workflow management system developed to improve coordination across sales, project management, production, and management teams within TheC Writers & Publishers.

The system was created to streamline business operations, improve visibility across departments, and reduce the inefficiencies associated with manual tracking processes.

The Problem

Before the system was introduced, key operational activities were managed manually.

Challenges included:

Difficulty tracking leads across the sales pipeline
Delays in monitoring project progress
Limited visibility into production activities
Inefficient communication between departments
Challenges in tracking client orders and job status

These issues increased administrative workload and reduced operational efficiency.

The Solution

TheC Workflow was designed as a centralized platform that allows teams to manage and monitor operational activities from a single system.

The platform provides structured workflows for lead tracking, project management, production monitoring, and client management.

This improves accountability, visibility, and collaboration across departments.

Business Impact

The system provides a structured framework for managing operational activities and helps improve efficiency by reducing reliance on manual tracking methods.

By centralising information and workflow processes, the platform supports better decision-making, improved team coordination, and more consistent project execution.
