Portfolio Case Study #1
Axon by Thec

Role: Head of Technology & Product Development
Status: Live

Overview

Axon is an AI-powered publishing and author-support platform developed to help African authors and aspiring authors overcome the challenges of traditional and self-publishing. The platform provides tools and resources that support users throughout the publishing journey, with particular emphasis on marketing, distribution, and long-term royalty generation.

The Problem

Many authors face significant barriers after completing their manuscripts. Common challenges include:

Limited access to publishing support
Difficulty marketing books effectively
Poor distribution channels
Limited visibility to readers
Inconsistent royalty earnings

These challenges often prevent talented authors from achieving commercial success and sustainable income from their work.

The Solution

Axon was designed as a technology-driven platform that helps authors move from manuscript development to publication, marketing, and distribution.

The platform combines publishing support, marketing systems, workflow automation, and AI-powered assistance to simplify the publishing process and improve opportunities for authors to earn from their intellectual property.
